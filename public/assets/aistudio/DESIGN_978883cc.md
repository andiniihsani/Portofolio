## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, literary minimalism balanced with gentle warmth and academic rigor. Designed for an ambitious Information Systems undergraduate and digital creator, the aesthetic merges contemporary editorial publishing with approachable digital craftsmanship. It eschews clinical, sterile tech tropes in favor of organic poise, tactile calm, and sophisticated intentionality.

### Audience & Mood
The primary audience includes university recruiters, scholarship committees, tech project partners, and design mentors. The interface must evoke:
- **Quiet confidence:** Restrained, well-proportioned typography and generous negative space that signal competence over noise.
- **Warmth and approachability:** Alabaster canvas tones and muted blush undertones that feel tactile and human.
- **Editorial precision:** Asymmetrical composition, high-contrast serif headlines, and delicate botanical touches that reflect deliberate visual curation.

### Design Movement
The design movement is **Minimalist Editorial** infused with soft tactile warmth. Characteristics include airy, content-first structures, generous border radiuses, delicate multi-point star flourishes (`✦`), structural arch masks for portraits, and dual-tone typographic hierarchies that convey academic weight alongside creative sensibility.

## Colors

The palette draws from natural stone, earthen pottery, and pressed parchment. It rejects standard digital primaries in favor of grounded, warm tones that create comfortable reading environments.

### Role Allocations
- **Primary (`#D38B78` - Terracotta):** Anchors primary call-to-actions, active links, leading glyphs, and accent headlines. It offers visual energy while harmonizing naturally with warm paper tones.
- **Secondary (`#545643` - Dark Olive):** Serves as high-contrast grounding element for auxiliary buttons, strong metadata chips, and editorial tags.
- **Tertiary (`#D8D8CE` - Warm Sage):** Forms soft container backgrounds, card tier separations, and secondary structural panels.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF6F2` - Alabaster Cream):** The base page background, establishing warmth and reducing harsh luminance contrast.

### Surface & Ink Hierarchy
1. **Surfaces:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards float atop the `#FAF6F2` canvas. Supplementary tint cards utilize `container-blush` (`#F1D9D1`) and `container-sand` (`#ECE4DC`) to delineate focus areas without harsh lines.
2. **Typography Inks:**
   - `ink-primary` (`#2D2724` - Deep Espresso): Used for high-impact serif titles, stat figures, and primary button labels.
   - `ink-secondary` (`#6E6560` - Muted Taupe): Used for sustained reading paragraphs, project narratives, and subtitles.
   - `ink-muted` (`#9A908A` - Warm Gray): Reserved for micro-eyebrows, dates, and subtle navigational metadata.

## Typography

The type system stages a dialogue between classical editorial sophistication and functional interface clarity.

### Typographic Roles
- **Display & Section Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Carries traditional publication authority. Employ italic variants selectively within headlines (e.g., *something extraordinary*) to provide rhythm and human personality. Hero headers use two-tone styling where the primary accent tint highlights lead concepts while deep espresso grounds the subtitle.
- **Interface & Narrative (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides modern geometric legibility at all text sizes. Clean ascenders and open apertures keep tabular metadata, metrics, and long-form bio descriptions effortless to digest.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Badges:** Rendered in `label-sm` or `label-md` with strict uppercase casing and extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`), accompanied by the terracotta sparkle glyph (`✦`).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a restrained **12-column responsive fluid grid** constrained to a maximum width of `1180px`. Generous vertical breathing space separates thematic sections, avoiding clutter and mirroring luxury print layouts.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (`≥ 1024px`):** Full 12-column layout with `5vw` outer margins and `6rem` (`96px`) section separation. Hero features an asymmetric 7:5 split (typography on the left, arch portrait on the right). Bento blocks align into 2 balanced columns.
- **Tablet (`768px – 1023px`):** Outer margin adjusts to `2rem`. 4-column cards collapse to a 2x2 grid. The floating metrics banner conforms to a balanced 2x2 matrix.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** Grid collapses to a single continuous column with `1.25rem` outer margins and `4rem` (`64px`) vertical section gaps. The hero portrait stacks below intro copy, and interactive buttons stretch across the view with a minimum hit height of `48px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy is established via soft atmospheric diffusion rather than severe, dark drops. The interface feels weightless, soft-edged, and gently tactile.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Base Canvas:** The flat `#FAF6F2` canvas anchors the scene with zero elevation.
- **Ambient Floating Cards:** High-contrast pure white panels receive a deeply diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 16px 36px -10px rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.06)`. On interactive hover states, this shifts to `0 20px 44px -8px rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.12)` with a slight `2px` upward translation.
- **Glassmorphic Navigation Bar:** Sticky top navigation utilizes a translucent layer (`rgba(250, 246, 242, 0.85)`) backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` and bounded underneath by an ultra-faint rule: `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.06)`.
- **Card Outlines:** Subtle, low-contrast borders (`1px solid rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.07)`) are layered with ambient shadows to maintain visual definition on low-gamut screens.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, approachable geometry governed by high roundedness tokens.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Pill System (`radius: 9999px`):** Every interactive button, filter tag, micro-label chip, and floating location indicator uses full-stadium pill geometry.
- **Card Architecture (`radius: 24px`):** Major content blocks, bento panels, and showcase containers implement a generous `24px` radius to reinforce tactile friendliness.
- **Asymmetrical Arch Motif:** Portrait media frames, selected decorative hero backgrounds, and featured image showcases use classical arched tops (`border-radius: 180px 180px 24px 24px`), introducing an editorial silhouette that breaks mechanical grid monotony.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in `#D38B78`, text in `#FFFFFF`, with `font-weight: 600`, `letter-spacing: 0.04em`, and padding of `14px 28px`. Hover shifts background to `#C27763` with an outward micro-translation of the trailing arrow glyph (`→`).
- **Secondary Pill:** Background in `#545643`, text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover darkens to `#434535`.
- **Ghost / Outline Pill:** Pure white or transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.2)` and text in `#2D2724`. Hover transitions to solid `#ECE4DC` with zero border contrast jump.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Eyebrows:** Upper-case micro-labels paired with a terracotta star (`✦`). Zero background, direct typography styling.
- **Pill Badges:** Soft sand (`#ECE4DC`) or blush (`#F1D9D1`) container fills with `8px 16px` padding and text set in `label-sm` (`#2D2724`). Used for skills (C++, Canva, Wix) and status roles.

### Cards & Bento Panels
- **Standard Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `24px` border radius, `1px solid rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.08)`, and `space-xl` internal padding.
- **Tinted Academic Bento:** Alternating background fills between soft blush (`#F1D9D1`) and muted warm sage (`#D8D8CE`), without shadows, utilizing border-free contrast against the main canvas.
- **Arch Portrait Card:** Framed visual container with arch mask, paired with a floating location badge (`BASED IN JAKARTA, ID`) anchored to the lower boundary.

### Interactive Form Inputs & Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Pill-shaped (`radius: 9999px`) or soft rounded (`16px`), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.12)`, text `#2D2724`, placeholder `#9A908A`.
- **Focus State:** Rings with `border-focus` (`rgba(211, 139, 120, 0.40)`) and an expansion box-shadow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(211, 139, 120, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Timeline Components
- **Education & Experience Timelines:** Minimal vertical tracking rule in `rgba(45, 39, 36, 0.12)`. Node markers are filled pills or small numbers (`01`, `02`) set in serif italic, establishing an academic dossier feel.
